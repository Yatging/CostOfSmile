## Feedback

- The registration system content is appropriate and has already been submitted.
- The core idea is strong and should continue.
- The theoretical research needs more depth and should include comparable artworks, not theory alone.
- Existing display ideas over-emphasise economic cost.
- Personal experience, bodily cost and social cost need visible forms: peer pressure, minor daily inconvenience and continuous self-management are currently missing.
- Sticky notes are insufficient as an artwork because they explain the inconvenience without transforming the experience.
- Raw treatment photographs would be too direct.
- The project can include digital and visual components; it does not need to remain a purely physical installation.
- A website should begin immediately to document effort and allow ongoing review.

## My response

I will preserve the economic layer while reorganising the project around three linked fields: **economic**, **bodily/personal** and **social** cost. I will begin from my existing data and objects, but will test ways to translate their underlying patterns rather than show them as unedited evidence.

## Next actions

1. Build and maintain this process archive.
2. Collect theory and relevant artworks in parallel.
3. Catalogue the aligner calendar and appointment conversations.
4. Ask the clinic about physical models, 3D scans and ClinCheck visualisations.
5. Develop initial digital/visual tests around time, repetition and compliance.
