A provisional GT registration entry was prepared so that the administrative process would not delay the project.

The working system choices included Prof. LAM Miu Ling as adviser, SM2706 and SM3804 as relevant courses, Contemporary Art as the main consultation area, and a broad summary that did not lock the project to one final installation form.

The first proposal was also reviewed critically. Key concerns included:

- the dollar sign may be too literal;
- the context wall may contain too much information;
- global cost data may not be directly comparable;
- U.S. and North American social research cannot simply stand in for Hong Kong;
- cleaning, mounting, lighting, ordering and privacy still require testing.
