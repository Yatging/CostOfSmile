Before the supervisor meeting, the proposal was rewritten around **body as project**.

The treatment process was understood as a sequence of scanning, modelling, numbered stages, scheduled changes, check-ups and a projected endpoint.

Supporting research was reorganised so that:

- medicalisation asks how treatment is framed through health and intervention;
- medical gaze addresses how the body becomes visible and measurable;
- dental surveillance addresses norms, records and self-monitoring;
- aesthetic research addresses the social meaning of an ideal smile;
- patient agency prevents the project from treating the patient as a passive victim.

The theoretical aim shifted from proving one explanation to examining how medical, commercial, aesthetic and personal forces overlap.
