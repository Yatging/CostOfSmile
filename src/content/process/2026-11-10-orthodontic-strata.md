## English

On 6 November, the rise and fall of the tooth cusps suggested two possibilities: a miniature landscape and a ring shaped from the outline of a single tooth. On 10 November, the landscape acquired a viewing sequence. From the side, the viewer first encounters time built up in layers. Looking down, they discover that the ridge curves into a dental arch. The ring idea remains in the [original process record](/CostOfSmile/process/2026-11-06-dental-landscapes-wearable-traces/).

Two material variations emerged. A packet left over from a change of trays could be folded into rocks along a bank. The colour of mouthwash could enter the foreground as a pool. Photographs from the same position will help test whether dry pleats suggest rake marks and whether real liquid adds anything that tinted film cannot convey.

**This milestone records the idea, the reference check and the fabrication plan.** The first task is to identify three to five retired stages and arrange anonymised side and overhead viewing tests. The prototype, viewer responses and photographs are still to come.

11 月 6 日看到牙尖起伏，想到微缩山水，也想到把单牙轮廓做成戒指。11 月 10 日，山水这条线多了一个观看顺序：观众在侧面先读到层层积起的时间，走近俯视，才发现山脊围成牙弓。戒指的想法继续保存在[原始记录](/CostOfSmile/process/2026-11-06-dental-landscapes-wearable-traces/)。

今天也提出把每次换副留下的包装袋折成岸石、把漱口水的颜色引入前景。干褶能否像耙纹、实液是否比一片透明色片多说了什么，都留给同机位对照测试。

**本日记录的是构想、文献核查和制作计划。** 首个待办是核对三至五个退役阶段，安排匿名的侧视与顶视测试。现阶段没有小样、观众反馈或现场照片。
