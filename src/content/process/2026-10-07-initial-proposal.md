The first proposal used the Invisalign treatment process as the starting point for an installation and archive.

The early display language included a dollar-sign form made from used aligners, numbered packaging, prop money, cost comparison, sticky notes about inconvenience and optional clinical imagery.

At this stage the project leaned strongly toward **economic cost** and the overlap between medical and consumer systems.

Prof. LAM Miu Ling had agreed to supervise the project. Follow-up communication focused on confirming receipt of the proposal, inviting comments and clarifying the School registration procedure.
