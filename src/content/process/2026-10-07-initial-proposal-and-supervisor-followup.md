## Initial direction

The first proposal treated my own Invisalign treatment as the starting point for an installation. The material archive already included used aligners and their numbered packaging, with clinical scans and progress records planned as additional sources.

The early conceptual emphasis was on the overlap between **medicalisation** and **consumerisation**: treatment could be discussed through health, appearance, payment, staged products and the promise of improvement.

## Early display language

The first installation study proposed:

- used aligners arranged into a **dollar-sign form**;
- a curved context wall using the original numbered packaging;
- prop money placed in packaging;
- comparative orthodontic-cost data;
- sticky notes describing daily inconvenience;
- optional clinical scans or progress photographs.

At this point, the visual language leaned heavily toward **economic cost**.

## Supervisor communication

Prof. LAM Miu Ling had agreed to supervise the project. A follow-up email was prepared to confirm that the proposal had arrived, ask for comments when convenient, and clarify the School's supervisor-registration procedure.

On 9 October, a short Instagram message was also prepared as a gentle delivery check rather than a demand for a reply.
