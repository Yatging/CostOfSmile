## 17 November 2026 — idea development, not fabrication

The [10 November Orthodontic Strata proposal](/CostOfSmile/process/2026-11-10-orthodontic-strata/) combined retired aligners as geological layers, packaging as a possible dry surface and mouthwash as a possible pool. Today the idea developed through three decisions:

1. Place two landscapes side by side: stained clear aligners with mouthwash on the left, and stained aligners with white toothpaste rake marks on the right.
2. Let viewers use toothbrushes to remove the mountain colour, exposing the transparent aligners. This connects the landscape to [Toothbrush Reveal](/CostOfSmile/experiments/toothbrush-reveal/) and makes repeated maintenance a proposed action.
3. Move the original packaging into a translucent background wall, ordered by treatment stage. The user confirmed this packaging-wall direction after discussing the visualization.

The new [Wet/Dry Orthodontic Landscape — Concept v2](/CostOfSmile/experiments/wet-dry-orthodontic-landscape/) records the arrangement, interpretation, proposed materials, unresolved feasibility questions, next prototypes and questions for Miu. It extends rather than replaces [Orthodontic Strata](/CostOfSmile/experiments/orthodontic-strata/).

**Evidence status:** concept and prototype plan only. A newly generated AI mockup is embedded in the experiment page and explicitly labelled. The earlier referenced image files were unavailable during this update; the new image is not represented as the previously approved original. No fabrication, public interaction, resolved material safety or supervisor approval is claimed. Dye migration, unknown plastic/coating compatibility, repainting, liquid containment, toothpaste ageing, privacy and structural maintenance remain tests to perform. Removing pigment is a metaphor, not a measure of real tooth-cleaning difficulty.
