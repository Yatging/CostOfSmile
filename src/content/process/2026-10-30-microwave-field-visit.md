Today I visited the opening of **Microwave International New Media Arts Festival 2026** at Hong Kong City Hall as part of the project's expansion from theoretical research into first-hand artwork references.

I photographed both the installation and its wall label. The label records **Miu Ling Lam, in collaboration with Cody Chow — _Light Years of Hope / 希望光年_ (2023)**. A dedicated [Research entry](/CostOfSmile/research/artwork-miu-ling-lam-light-years-of-hope/) now records the field documentation and focuses on how an intangible spoken wish is translated into light and sound rather than displayed as raw data.

The visit reinforced a useful principle for Cost of Smile:

> **Do not only show the archive. Translate what the archive does to the body.**

On the same day, a new everyday-life idea emerged while eating a pale soy-milk-sauce pasta: an **Aligner-Friendly Food Chart**. The [experiment note](/CostOfSmile/experiments/aligner-friendly-food-chart/) begins with colour / transparency and sugar as intuitive variables, then opens up additional questions around staining and residue, cleaning burden, hardness / attachment risk, extra cleaning, and the friction that treatment adds to spontaneous eating.

This remains a **personal artistic taxonomy**, not a medical or nutritional food guide.
