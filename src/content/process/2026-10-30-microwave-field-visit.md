Today I visited the opening of **Microwave International New Media Arts Festival 2026** at Hong Kong City Hall as part of the project's expansion from theoretical research into first-hand artwork references.

A dedicated Research entry now records **Miu Ling Lam and Cody Chow's _Light Years of Hope_**, focusing on how a spoken, intangible input is translated into light and sound rather than displayed as raw data.

The visit reinforced a useful principle for Cost of Smile:

> **Do not only show the archive. Translate what the archive does to the body.**

On the same day, a new everyday-life idea emerged while eating a pale soy-milk-sauce pasta: an **Aligner-Friendly Food Chart** that could classify meals by colour, sugar, hardness, residue, cleaning effort and interruption to wear time. It remains a brainstorm / prototype direction rather than a medical food guide.
