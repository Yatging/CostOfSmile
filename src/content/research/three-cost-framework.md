The project now uses three connected layers as a working research framework.

## Economic cost

Fees, branded packaging, staged products and the market promise of correction.

## Bodily and personal cost

Pain, wear time, maintenance, eating and cleaning routines, appointments, waiting and the repeated labour of compliance.

## Social cost

Peer pressure, beauty norms, judgement and the internalised expectation to present an improved smile.

The categories are not final chapters. They overlap. Money purchases a system of bodily discipline; bodily discomfort is accepted because a future social image has already been imagined. Further theory and artwork references will test and complicate this framework.
