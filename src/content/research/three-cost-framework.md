The project now uses three connected layers as a working research framework.

## Economic cost

Fees, branded packaging, staged products and the market promise of correction.

This layer connects most directly to consumerisation and to the medical-commercial visual language already present in the packaging and treatment system.

## Bodily and personal cost

Wear time, maintenance, eating and cleaning routines, appointments, waiting, failed attachments and the repeated labour of compliance.

This layer connects to **body as project**, dental self-monitoring and the daily experience of treatment.

## Social cost

Peer pressure, beauty norms, judgement and the internalised expectation to present an improved smile.

This layer is currently the least locally researched and therefore needs additional Hong Kong / East Asian material.

## Why the three layers should overlap

The categories are not final chapters.

Money purchases a system of bodily discipline. Bodily inconvenience may be accepted because a future social image has already been imagined. A patient can be socially influenced while still exercising agency.

The framework therefore operates as a map for experiments, not as three isolated messages.
