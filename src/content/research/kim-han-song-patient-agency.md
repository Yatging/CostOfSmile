Kim, Han and Song provide a useful counterweight to a purely disciplinary reading of dentistry by giving greater attention to patient agency, self-determination, health literacy and care of the self.

## How this informs Cost of Smile

I do not want the project to present me as a passive victim of a dentist, a market or a beauty norm.

My treatment contains several forces at once:

- I received professional advice;
- price and a clinic discount mattered;
- aesthetic expectations mattered;
- I nevertheless chose to begin treatment;
- I perform the daily labour required to continue it.

This makes **power and agency operating together** more useful than a simple story of external control.

## Limits / caution

Agency should not be used to erase social pressure or economic structure. Likewise, power should not be used to erase the fact that patients can actively desire and value treatment.
