## Work / context — 作品与背景

In a 2014 *Art Jewelry Forum* interview, Katja Prins discusses her *Hybrids* jewellery works made using materials such as chromium-plated brass and resin. The series considers the porous boundary between bodies and technologies, especially the shift from medical technologies that repair illness toward those that seek enhancement. Prins mentions visits to a dental laboratory as an **indirect influence**, not as proof that this series is made from used aligners. The interview includes photographs of individual brooches and a necklace.

## What can inform Cost of Smile? — 与本项目的联系

The important connection is not a literal tooth shape but **ambivalence about enhancement**. Orthodontics can be a chosen act of health care while also being affected by aesthetic expectations. Prins's pieces hold fascination and discomfort together; a wearable trace of an aligner could similarly refuse a simple pro-/anti-treatment statement. 中文：首饰既是美化物，也是对身体改造技术的提问。

## A specific experiment to try — 可执行的借鉴

Develop a small replica-mounted piece that can be read both as a jewelled object and a bodily/medical trace. Photograph it on a display stand rather than assuming it must be worn; ask whether adding ornament makes treatment appear glamorous, uncanny, or both.

## Distinction / limits — 需要避免的误读

**Do not describe Hybrids as jewellery made of dental aligners.** The cited pieces are sculptural art jewellery; the relationship to Cost of Smile is conceptual, not material. Material safety and body contact require separate testing.
