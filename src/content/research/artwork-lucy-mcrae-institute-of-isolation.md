## Work / medium

**Institute of Isolation** is an observational science-fiction film and speculative training environment. McRae imagines a near-future institution that conditions the body for isolation, sensory deprivation, altered gravity and extreme environments.

## What the work does

The project does not simply state that bodies adapt under technological systems. It invents a coherent **visual institution**—chambers, training equipment, clothing, rituals and protocols—that lets the viewer experience the logic of bodily conditioning.

## Why it matters to Cost of Smile

This is useful for thinking beyond a conventional installation plus text panel.

Orthodontic routines—wearing, removing, brushing, changing stage, attending appointments—could be translated into a fictional or semi-clinical system of training tools, interfaces and instructions.

The **Toothbrush Reveal** experiment already moves in this direction: an everyday dental-care action becomes a designed interaction through which hidden cost is disclosed.

## Formal method to borrow

- build a coherent visual world around bodily discipline;
- turn instruction and routine into designed objects or interfaces;
- combine film, prop, environment and speculative language;
- let the audience encounter a system rather than read an explanation.

## Caution

McRae's work uses high-production science-fiction aesthetics. Cost of Smile should not become a generic futuristic set. The strongest material remains my real archive; speculative elements should emerge from actual orthodontic routines and records.
