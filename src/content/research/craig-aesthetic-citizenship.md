Craig's work is useful because it shows that a “perfect smile” can acquire social meanings beyond oral health.

For Cost of Smile, the study is treated as one documented social context rather than universal evidence.

Because the article is U.S.-focused, further Hong Kong or East Asian research is needed if social pressure, class and belonging remain central to the artwork.
