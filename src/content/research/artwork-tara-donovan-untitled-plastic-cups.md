## Artwork / site — 作品背景

Pace Gallery identifies Donovan's *Untitled (Plastic Cups)* (2006) as an installation formed by stacking plastic cups into a terrain reminiscent of rolling hills or waves. The Institute of Contemporary Art/Boston describes the immense plastic-cup landscape in its exhibition publication. Both sources illustrate the contrast between everyday industrial units and their collective organic appearance.

## Relevance to Cost of Smile — 关联与分析

Accumulation is the formal reference: **unit → repetition → topography**. Each clear aligner is similarly industrially fabricated, numbered and temporary, but the meaning comes from its relation to a particular body and treatment sequence. This project has far fewer units, so spacing and light must do more work. 中文：大量相似的塑胶物件能形成自然地形，但你的牙套数量与形态限制不同。

## Method / test to borrow — 可尝试的方法

Photograph three configurations using the same five aligners: widely spaced chronology, dense repeated pile and elevated profile. Compare the legibility of the individual units and the landscape effect. Record failures rather than assuming accumulation will automatically look like hills.

## Limits — 区别与注意

Donovan's work is not about dentistry and depends on an enormous quantity of materials. *Cost of Smile* should not imitate scale by inventing discarded aligners or concealing that there are only a limited number of stages.
