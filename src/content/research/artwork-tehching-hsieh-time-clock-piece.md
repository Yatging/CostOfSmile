## Work / medium

**One Year Performance 1980–1981 (Time Clock Piece)** is a year-long performance in which Hsieh punched a mechanical time clock every hour, on the hour. M+ records the work through time cards, statements, photographs, film, the clock itself and other documentary material.

## What the work does

The artwork is not an image of discipline; it is a **rule lived through time**. The hourly punch reorganises sleeping, movement and daily life around a repetitive protocol. Its administrative evidence—the cards, statements and timestamps—becomes the surviving visual form.

## Why it matters to Cost of Smile

This is one of the clearest references for my aligner-change calendar and daily wear-time requirement.

Orthodontic self-management also operates through repeated small deadlines: wear time, stage changes, cleaning, appointments and compliance. The calendar can therefore become more than evidence. It can become a visual system that makes the imposed rhythm of treatment perceptible.

## Formal method to borrow

- let a rule generate the archive;
- make repetition visible through accumulated records;
- use administrative aesthetics rather than decorative illustration;
- compress a long duration into a legible visual structure.

## Caution

Hsieh deliberately pushes discipline to an extreme. Invisalign is not comparable in severity. The useful reference is **form and method**—time, repetition, self-monitoring—not an equivalence of experience.
