## Artwork / site — 作品背景

Stackonis describes *RETAINING WALLS* as an artist-book installation rooted in family labour history in Pennsylvania's coal region, resource extraction and chronic pain. Its etched images are informed by observed dental moulds and cast impressions. The imagery invites comparison between human teeth, damaged land and the social and environmental consequences of extraction. The artist's own website includes work photographs and a detailed statement.

## Relevance to Cost of Smile — 关联与分析

The material link is **dental impressions ↔ landscapes**, but the more important link is ethical: a landscape is not just a beautiful form; it also records maintenance, extraction and embodied cost. *Cost of Smile* can ask what is moved or 'corrected' when the body is put through a planned sequence, while retaining the personal specificity of orthodontic wear and daily cleaning. 中文：身体与地景的相似性既有形态的一面，也可以讨论劳动和代价。

## Method / test to borrow — 可尝试的方法

Combine an early and late aligner with separate label cards recording actual stage/time and one verified daily-care action. Try an understated 'strata' display rather than a decorative miniature with invented geological history. Record whether viewers notice the dental source.

## Limits — 区别与注意

Stackonis's focus on mining, family histories and chronic pain should not be flattened into a generic 'teeth look like mountains' reference. The project's dental casts are **not** examples of used clear-aligner installation; our reading is a distinct application.
