## Work / medium

**The 2009 Feltron Annual Report** is a printed report using letterpress and information design to visualise a year of Felton's quantifiable experiences. MoMA notes that the 2009 report incorporated data from people with whom he had substantial face-to-face encounters, including both factual and subjective observations.

## What the work does

The report borrows the authoritative visual language of corporate reporting and applies it to intimate life. Accumulated fragments become a narrative precisely because the design treats them as a system.

## Why it matters to Cost of Smile

My orthodontic treatment already comes with a corporate-medical visual language: stage numbers, packages, dates, appointments and planned progress.

Felton suggests a way to **appropriate the system's own aesthetics** instead of placing an explanatory text beside the objects. A “treatment report” could make the administrative logic visible while still remaining autobiographical.

## Formal method to borrow

- use serial data to build a narrative;
- appropriate institutional / corporate graphic language;
- combine quantitative and subjective data;
- treat layout itself as conceptual material.

## Caution

Felton's work can make self-tracking feel elegant and controlled. My treatment archive should also preserve failure, inconvenience and incomplete sequences rather than presenting a perfectly managed self.
