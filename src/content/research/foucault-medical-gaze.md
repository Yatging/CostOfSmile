Foucault's **medical gaze** is useful for thinking about how the body becomes legible through specialist knowledge.

In *The Birth of the Clinic*, modern clinical knowledge is connected to new ways of observing, describing, classifying and mapping the body. For Cost of Smile, the concept matters because orthodontics generates a specialised visual body: scans, digital models, measurements, comparisons and projected movement.

## How this informs Cost of Smile

The intraoral scan does not merely “take a picture” of my teeth. It translates the mouth into a form that can be inspected, compared, measured and planned.

This is particularly relevant to the possible **Digital Body** experiment:

**physical mouth → scan → digital model → planned future position**

## Limits / caution

The medical gaze should not be simplified into “the dentist is watching me.” It concerns a system of medical perception and knowledge. For everyday routines, schedules and self-monitoring, Nettleton's work on dentistry is the closer reference.
