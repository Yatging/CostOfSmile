The medical gaze is useful for thinking about how the body becomes legible through specialist knowledge.

In this project, the relevant forms are concrete: intraoral scans, digital models, measurements, comparison and diagnosis.

The concept is not used to mean that a clinician literally watches the patient continuously. For everyday monitoring and self-discipline in dentistry, Sarah Nettleton provides the closer reference.
