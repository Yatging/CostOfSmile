## Work / context — 作品与背景

*Dimensions Variable* was a **two-person exhibition** of David Alban and Julie Anne Ward at Artspace Loveland Gallery (18 September–2 October 2017), curated by Amy Joy Hosterman. The exhibition text describes Alban's ceramic forms based on teeth and mandibles, developed in response to extensive dental treatment. It also describes his graphite drawings cut and reassembled into repeated, layered, rock-like landscapes. **Dimensions Variable is the exhibition title, not a verified title of one individual Alban artwork.**

## What can inform Cost of Smile? — 与本项目的联系

This is unusually close to the idea of **body contours as geological terrain**. The artist does not simply decorate an object with mountain imagery: the dental experience informs the form, while collage and repeated fragments translate an intimate body-scale structure into something resembling land. 中文：从口腔的形状转移到地质的层理，让身体变化通过地景可见。

## A specific experiment to try — 可执行的借鉴

Try two studies side by side: (A) unaltered aligners layered with spacers, keeping their 3D tooth impressions; (B) traced arch contours cut from safe replacement sheet material and layered like topographic profiles. Compare which gives a more convincing mountain **without erasing the fact that the source is a tooth**.

## Distinction / limits — 需要避免的误读

This source does not establish that Alban used discarded Invisalign trays or that he produced the exact same 'orthodontic strata' method. Cite the exhibition accurately, and identify the work's connection to our installation as a formal analogy rather than identical materials.
