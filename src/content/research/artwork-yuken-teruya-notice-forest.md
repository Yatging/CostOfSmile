## Artwork / site — 作品背景

The Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo records Teruya's *Notice – Forest: Madison Avenue* (2011) as a work made from **paper bag and glue**, photographed by Shizune Shiigi. Viewers discover a delicate tree inside an ordinary branded bag. The artist's studio presents other works from *Notice-Forest*. The material's commercial function is part of the image: it was once packaging and evokes the tree from which paper was made.

## Relevance to Cost of Smile — 关联与分析

This offers a precise method for the **backdrop of numbered aligner packets**: preserve visible material history and use minimal intervention to produce a different scene. Rather than adding an unrelated decorative landscape, a wrapper can become the landscape surface or framing device. 中文：包装袋不只是背景板，更可以成为转化中的主体材料。

## Method / test to borrow — 可尝试的方法

Make removable test folds from **duplicate packaging**, comparing curved pleats resembling raked water, parallel pleats and a cut opening that frames a dental 'rock'. Photograph in light and dark conditions. Keep identifying names and appointment details covered unless deliberately approved for public display.

## Limits — 区别与注意

The referenced work uses *paper*, not thin plastic orthodontic film. A paper tree's folds and structural stiffness cannot establish that plastic wrappers will stand on their own. Test mechanical stability and visible print before committing to a final arrangement.
