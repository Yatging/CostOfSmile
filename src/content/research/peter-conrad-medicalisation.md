Medicalisation is used here as a question rather than a verdict.

For Cost of Smile, the useful issue is how dental difference can be described through language such as diagnosis, bite, risk and oral function while treatment may also promise aesthetic improvement.

The project therefore avoids the earlier, stronger claim that orthodontics is simply a clear case of medicalisation.
