Nettleton brings Foucauldian questions directly into dentistry.

For Cost of Smile, surveillance refers to norms, records and schedules that encourage self-monitoring rather than literal continuous observation.

Stage numbers, change dates, expected wear time, cleaning routines and check-ups are useful because they show how treatment enters everyday behaviour.
