Nettleton brings Foucauldian questions of surveillance, monitoring and normalisation directly into dentistry.

WorldCat's summary of *Power, Pain and Dentistry* identifies the book as a genealogy of dentistry concerned with relations between dental power and knowledge, drawing on disciplinary power, surveillance, monitoring and normalisation.

## How this informs Cost of Smile

The most useful point for Invisalign is **self-monitoring**.

The dentist is not literally watching me throughout the day. Instead, treatment supplies norms and records that I internalise:

- expected wear time;
- scheduled stage changes;
- check-ups;
- cleaning routines;
- restrictions around eating;
- comparison with a planned outcome.

This is why calendar data and repeated small inconveniences matter artistically: they show how the treatment system enters ordinary behaviour.

## Limits / caution

The work should avoid turning every dental routine into oppression. Self-discipline can also be willingly accepted as part of care, improvement and patient agency.
