The artwork-reference search is organised around five clusters:

1. medical archives and bodily traces;
2. personal data, calendars and self-tracking;
3. medical imaging, scans and digital bodies;
4. beauty, consumption, peer pressure and social norms;
5. repetitive care, maintenance and self-discipline.

Individual case studies will be added only after a reliable artist, museum, gallery or institutional source has been confirmed. The aim is to study **artistic method and form**, not only conceptual similarity.
