The first proposal recorded daily inconvenience through sticky notes. The content remains important even though the sticky-note form is no longer considered strong enough.

Current experiences to catalogue include:

- removing aligners before eating;
- brushing and cleaning before reinsertion;
- limits on spontaneous eating;
- keeping track of removed aligners;
- avoiding hard food because small components can fail;
- monitoring wear time;
- changing to the next stage on schedule.

The next task is to record these experiences more systematically and translate their **repetition, duration and friction** into visual, digital, sonic or interactive form.
