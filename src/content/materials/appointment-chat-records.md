The appointment conversations reveal treatment as a repeated negotiation with time: reminders, questions, availability, delays, travel and return visits.

## Handling and privacy

Raw screenshots will not be published automatically. Names, avatars, phone numbers, clinic staff details and unrelated conversation must be removed. The artistic focus is the pattern of timestamps and recurring administrative language, not the exposure of a private conversation.

## Possible translations

- Recompose only timestamps and appointment verbs into a typographic sequence.
- Recreate the rhythm of notification and response in a restrained digital interface.
- Map delay, waiting and rescheduling as gaps in a visual score.
