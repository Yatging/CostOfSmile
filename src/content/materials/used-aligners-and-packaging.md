Each used aligner is a temporary mould of the body at one moment in treatment. Together, the objects form a sequence of discarded stages: intimate bodily residues organised through industrial numbering and packaging.

The material can address economic cost, but its seriality also speaks to time, discipline and the idea of the body as a planned project.

Future documentation will record stage number, dates of use, condition and any deviation from the treatment schedule.
