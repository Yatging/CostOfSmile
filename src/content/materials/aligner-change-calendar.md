The calendar is more than administrative evidence. Each entry links a date to a numbered treatment stage, showing how the body is organised in advance through a sequence of small deadlines.

## Possible translations

- A long visual timeline in which dates accumulate into treatment duration.
- A counter connecting stage numbers to lived days.
- A moving or generative display that makes the gap between planned and actual change visible.
- A repeated notification structure that recreates the pressure of compliance.

Before public use, the source calendar will be checked for unrelated personal information.
