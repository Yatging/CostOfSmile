The clinic may be able to provide dental models, intraoral scans or treatment-simulation material.

If access and permission are confirmed, these sources could create a strong three-part relation:

- **used aligner = physical residue of a past bodily state**
- **3D scan = body translated into measurable digital data**
- **predicted final model = future body defined before it physically exists**

This direction is especially relevant to **body as project** because a future body can be visualised and managed as a sequence before treatment is complete.

Raw clinical imagery should not automatically be displayed as documentary proof; it needs visual translation or integration.
