Appointment records reveal another kind of treatment labour: arranging time.

Instead of directly publishing raw conversation screenshots, the material can be translated through timestamps, reminder language, delay, waiting, rescheduling and repeated return visits.

Possible outputs include a restrained notification interface, a typographic score or a timeline of waiting and return.

The artistic focus is the **rhythm of medical administration**, not the exposure of private conversation.
