Several times during treatment, eating hard food caused a small white attachment to detach.

The incident is minor in scale but large in consequence: one missing component can interrupt fit, require another appointment and add time to the treatment routine.

## Direction A — hidden object

A small white attachment could be hidden somewhere in the installation with only a light clue such as:

> Something is missing.

or

> I lost this more than once.

The viewer discovers the failure only after looking carefully.

## Direction B — alignment interaction

A larger dental model and transparent prop aligner could ask viewers to position the aligner onto the correct attachments.

One attachment could deliberately be absent. The aligner then refuses to fit, making the viewer realise that the problem is not simply their own incorrect action.

## Core question

**Can a tiny missing component make the hidden maintenance of orthodontic treatment physically understandable to the viewer?**

The incident can connect a chain of evidence:

hard food → detached attachment → treatment disruption → appointment → clinic visit → time cost.
