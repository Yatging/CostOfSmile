## Concept

Retired clear aligners become miniature mountains in two adjacent landscapes. On the left, stained aligners and mouthwash form a **wet shanshui / 真山水**. On the right, stained aligners rise from **white toothpaste rake marks**, recalling a dry garden / 枯山水. The terrain brings the repeated work of bodily care into the landscape.

The work develops [Orthodontic Strata](/CostOfSmile/experiments/orthodontic-strata/), treating successive aligners as layers of time, and connects it with the brushing gesture of [Toothbrush Reveal](/CostOfSmile/experiments/toothbrush-reveal/).

## Arrangement

- **Left / wet:** overlapping, stained clear aligners form rock faces and mountain ridges. A shallow vessel holds mouthwash as a river or lake.
- **Right / dry:** similar aligner mountains sit among white toothpaste ridges combed into concentric and flowing lines.
- **Behind both:** original aligner packaging sachets, ordered by treatment stage, form a translucent medical archive wall. Gaps mark missing stages; anonymous stage codes carry the sequence.
- **In front:** a brushing station holds toothbrushes and replaceable mountain samples, with a tray to collect colour residue.

## Viewer interaction

A visitor uses a supplied toothbrush to gradually brush colour from a mountain surface, revealing the transparent aligner and its tooth-shaped cavities. A partly revealed surface allows the landscape and bodily object to be seen together.

The first prototype uses replaceable experimental pieces and safe pigments. The station includes residue collection, dedicated display brushes, and a cleaning and replacement routine.

## Interpretation

Gradual tooth movement becomes geological change. Mouthwash and toothpaste enter two contrasting landscape traditions, while the packaging wall gives bodily change the rhythm of a numbered medical archive.

Brushing away colour is an **artistic metaphor for the repetition and difficulty of daily care**. The viewer's movement brings attention to time, effort and the ongoing maintenance behind an ideal smile.

## Proposed materials

Retired clear aligners and clean replicas; original anonymised packaging sachets; removable pigments or coatings; mouthwash; toothpaste; toothbrushes; replaceable sample carriers; residue trays; reversible cradles or clamps; a stable plinth; and a translucent archive frame.

Material comparisons include simulated water surfaces and stable toothpaste-like media.

## Feasibility and limitations

1. **Colour migration:** test pigment contact with mouthwash and toothpaste in separate sample vessels. Record changes in colour, texture and residue.
2. **Plastics and joins:** identify the aligner material and compare adhesive compatibility, coating removal and reversible supports on sample pieces. Photograph scratching, haze and staining.
3. **Wear and resetting:** measure usable brushing cycles, recolouring and drying time, sample replacement and support repairs.
4. **Mouthwash:** check product composition, alcohol content, evaporation, odour, hygiene and venue requirements. Test vessel compatibility, leak capture and separation from electrical equipment and the brushing station.
5. **Toothpaste:** track hardening, cracking, tackiness, dust and rake-pattern changes over time. Compare replaceable toothpaste trays with a stable sculptural medium.
6. **Participation:** use replaceable pieces and safe pigments; organise residue collection, brush cleaning, reach and access.
7. **Objects and privacy:** establish a material-compatible cleaning process for retired aligners. Mask names, case numbers, clinic identifiers, barcodes and QR codes on packaging; check front, reverse, backlit and photographed views.
8. **Structure and upkeep:** test plinth stability under brushing, vessel movement, frame support, edge conditions and cleaning access. Record daily inspection, reset and maintenance time.

## Next prototype steps

1. Inventory a small set of retired stages and packaging. Record material, condition and anonymous stage codes, and keep an untouched comparison sample.
2. Prepare a matrix of coatings on replaceable pieces. Record drying time, brush type, strokes, colour removal, scratches, residue and reset time. Photograph each result.
3. Trial pigment contact with mouthwash and toothpaste separately. Record colour, texture, toothpaste ridges and liquid level after 24 hours, then extend the observation to the exhibition timescale.
4. Build a small wet/dry composition and a packaging-wall fragment. Test mounting, brushing forces, containment, reach and cleaning access.
5. Arrange a small supervised viewer trial. Ask what viewers notice before and after brushing and how they connect the gesture to repeated care.
6. Add dated observations, costs, repair frequency and photographs to the archive, and discuss the next iteration with Miu.

## Supervisor feedback questions

- How does the wet/dry contrast bring bodily labour into the landscape?
- Where should brushing take place: on the main mountains or on replaceable samples beside them?
- How can the packaging wall preserve the specificity of my treatment while protecting personal information?
- What do real mouthwash and toothpaste contribute compared with simulated materials?
- How can the first prototype strengthen the relationship between geological change, tooth movement and repeated care?

### Concept / 概念

以退役透明牙套组成微缩双联山水：左侧是染色牙套与漱口水构成的真山水；右侧是染色牙套与牙膏白色耙纹构成的枯山水。地貌与牙齿都在缓慢变化，景观中的维护动作回应日常身体护理。

作品延伸自 [Orthodontic Strata / 口中地层](/CostOfSmile/experiments/orthodontic-strata/)，将不同阶段的牙套视为时间地层，并结合 [Toothbrush Reveal](/CostOfSmile/experiments/toothbrush-reveal/) 的刷拭揭示动作。

### Arrangement / 布置

- **左侧真山水：** 染色牙套叠成岩石与山脊，浅槽中的漱口水形成河湖。
- **右侧枯山水：** 牙套山体立于牙膏耙出的白色环形与流动纹路之中。
- **包装档案墙：** 原始牙套包装袋按治疗阶段排列，组成半透明医疗档案背墙。缺期留空，以匿名阶段编号呈现序列。
- **刷拭区：** 前方放置牙刷、可替换山体试片及收集颜色残渣的托盘。

### Viewer interaction / 观众互动

观众使用提供的牙刷，逐渐刷去山体表层颜色，显露透明牙套及牙齿形状。部分褪色的山体让“山水”与“身体用品”同时可见。

首轮小样采用可替换试片与安全颜料，并设置残渣收集、专用展示牙刷及清洁更换流程。

### Interpretation / 阐释

牙齿移动转译为地质变化，漱口水与牙膏进入两种对照的山水语言。包装编号呈现治疗的秩序，刷拭则让日常维护劳动显现。

**刷褪颜色是反复护理及其困难的艺术隐喻。** 观众的动作把时间、付出与持续维护带入对理想笑容的观看。

### Proposed materials / 拟用材料

退役牙套与干净复制件、匿名化原包装袋、可移除颜料或涂层、漱口水、牙膏、牙刷、可替换试片托架、残渣盘、可逆支撑或夹具、稳固展台及半透明档案墙支架。

材料对照包括模拟水面与稳定的仿牙膏造型材料。

### Feasibility and limitations / 可行性与限制

1. **颜色迁移：** 分开测试颜料与漱口水、牙膏的接触，记录色泽、质地和残渣变化。
2. **牙套与连接：** 识别牙套材质，在试片上比较胶黏兼容性、涂层移除与可逆支撑，拍摄划痕、雾化和染色。
3. **磨损与复位：** 记录刷拭次数、补色、干燥、换片及支撑修复所需时间。
4. **漱口水：** 检查配方、酒精含量、蒸发、气味、卫生与场地要求，测试容器兼容、接漏及与电器和互动区的隔离。
5. **牙膏：** 观察硬化、开裂、发黏、积尘与耙纹变化，对比可更换牙膏盘和稳定造型材料。
6. **互动管理：** 使用可换试片与安全颜料，安排残渣收集、牙刷清洁、触及高度和操作空间。
7. **清洁与隐私：** 制定与牙套材质兼容的清洁流程；遮蔽包装姓名、病例号、诊所信息、条码与二维码，并检查正反面、透光和照片中的显示。
8. **结构与维护：** 检验刷拭力下展台稳定、容器移动、背墙支撑、边缘及清洁通道，记录日常检查、复位和维修时间。

### Next prototype steps / 下一轮小样

1. 清点少量退役阶段与包装，记录材质、原状和匿名编号，保留未处理对照样本。
2. 制作可换片涂层矩阵，记录干燥时间、刷型、次数、脱色、划伤、残渣与复位时间，拍摄各组结果。
3. 分开测试颜料与漱口水、牙膏的接触，记录 24 小时后的颜色、质地、耙纹和液位，再延长至展期尺度。
4. 制作小型双联山水及包装墙片段，测试安装、受力、接漏、触及和清洁。
5. 安排小规模监督观看测试，记录刷前刷后的理解，以及观众如何把动作与重复护理联系起来。
6. 按日期归档观察、成本、修复频率和照片，与 Miu 讨论下一轮调整。
