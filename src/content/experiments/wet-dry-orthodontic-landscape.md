## Concept

Retired clear aligners become miniature mountains in two adjacent landscapes. On the left, stained clear aligners and mouthwash form a **wet shanshui / 真山水**, with real liquid proposed as the water. On the right, stained aligners rise from **toothpaste arranged into white rake marks**, recalling a dry garden / 枯山水. The artificial terrain needs repeated care, just as a changing dental arch requires sustained maintenance.

This extends [Orthodontic Strata](/CostOfSmile/experiments/orthodontic-strata/), retaining treatment stages as layers of time, and brings in the repeated revealing gesture from [Toothbrush Reveal](/CostOfSmile/experiments/toothbrush-reveal/). Both earlier experiments remain part of the archive.

## Arrangement

- **Left / wet:** overlapping, stained clear aligners suggest rock faces and mountains. A separate shallow vessel proposes mouthwash as a river or lake. The visual relationship between mountains and liquid must be tested without assuming direct contact is viable.
- **Right / dry:** similar aligner mountains sit among white toothpaste ridges combed into concentric and flowing lines. “Dry” names the garden imagery; it does not mean toothpaste is physically dry or stable.
- **Behind both:** original aligner packaging sachets, ordered by verified treatment stage, form a translucent medical archive wall. Preserve gaps where stages are missing. Packaging records the sequence of bodily change and the system that numbers it. Anonymous stage codes must replace exposed personal information.
- **In front:** a clearly bounded brushing station with replaceable samples, separate from the liquid and archival originals, is the first test arrangement. Access to the main mountains is a later decision.

## Viewer interaction

The proposed encounter begins with terrain. A visitor uses a supplied toothbrush to gradually brush colour from a mountain surface, revealing the transparent aligner and its tooth-shaped cavities. A partly revealed surface allows the landscape and bodily object to be seen together.

First test the gesture on replaceable experimental pieces using an appropriately assessed, low-risk pigment. “Washable” or “non-toxic” alone does not establish suitability for this particular contact and display situation. Collect residue; define brush replacement and cleaning procedures before participation. Display brushes are never for oral use. Keep real mouthwash away from the interactive station during the first trials.

## Interpretation

The changing terrain connects gradual tooth movement to geological change. The wet/dry pair places two everyday care consumables into contrasting landscape traditions. The packaging wall makes treatment look like an ordered archive, while brushing introduces the repetitive labour that this neat sequence can hide.

Brushing off colour is an **artistic metaphor for repeated maintenance and its difficulty**. It is not equivalent to cleaning teeth, and removal time or resistance cannot measure dental hygiene, treatment effort or clinical effectiveness. Viewer readings may differ; record them rather than claiming the interaction necessarily produces empathy.

## Proposed materials

Retired clear aligners and/or clean replicas; original anonymised packaging sachets; removable pigments or coatings selected after testing; mouthwash in a separately contained test vessel; real toothpaste for short material trials; toothbrushes; replaceable sample carriers; residue trays; reversible cradles or clamps; a stable plinth; and a supported translucent archive frame. Simulated water and a stable toothpaste-like medium remain comparison options, explicitly labelled if used. None is a confirmed final material specification.

## Feasibility and limitations

These are **open experiments, not solved technical details**:

1. **Colour migration:** dye or pigment may contaminate mouthwash and toothpaste, changing colour, texture and behaviour. Keep separate controls and test compatibility in isolated sample vessels; do not assume a coating remains on the mountain.
2. **Unknown plastics and joins:** the actual aligner material, adhesive compatibility and coating reversibility are unverified. Bonding or brushing may haze, scratch or permanently stain the plastic. Begin with sacrificial pieces or replicas and reversible supports, not valuable originals.
3. **Wear and resetting:** repeated brushing consumes the colour layer and may abrade the substrate. Recolouring, drying, replacing samples and repairing supports will be part of maintenance. Measure reset time and usable cycles.
4. **Real mouthwash:** leaks, evaporation, alcohol content where present, odour, hygiene and venue requirements remain unresolved. Check the exact product information and venue conditions; trial compatible containment, spill capture and separation from electricity and visitors. The pictured open water must not be read as proof of exhibition safety. Compare a simulated surface if real liquid is impractical.
5. **Real toothpaste:** hardening, cracking, tackiness, dust and loss of the rake pattern may make it unsuitable for prolonged open display. Record changes over time and compare a replaceable short-duration tray with a clearly labelled stable substitute.
6. **Participation:** first use replaceable test pieces and assessed safe pigments, with residue containment and a handling plan. No public brushing trial has yet established acceptable mess, exposure, accessibility or maintenance.
7. **Used objects and privacy:** agree on a compatible cleaning and hygiene process for retired aligners before display; use clean replicas where that cannot be established. Mask names, case numbers, clinic identifiers, barcodes and QR codes on packaging, including reverse, backlit and photographed views. Stage order should not expose identity.
8. **Structure and upkeep:** test plinth stability under brushing, vessel movement, frame tipping, reachable heights, edge conditions and cleaning access. The packaging wall and liquid require independent support and a practicable daily inspection/reset plan.

## Next prototype steps

1. Inventory a small set of retired stages and packaging; document consent, anonymisation, condition and material uncertainty. Keep an untouched comparison sample.
2. Prepare replaceable sample pieces with a small matrix of coatings. Record coating, drying time, brush type, strokes, visible removal, scratches, residue and reset time. Photograph failures as well as successes.
3. Separately trial pigment contact with mouthwash and toothpaste. Compare before/after colour and texture. Record toothpaste ridges and liquid level over 24 hours, then extend only if initial results justify a longer test matching the proposed display period.
4. Build a small wet/dry composition and an anonymised packaging-wall fragment. Test reversible mounting, brushing forces, containment, reach and cleaning access. Compare real and simulated consumables with their identities visible in the record.
5. Invite a small supervised viewer trial only after the material and handling plan is acceptable. Ask what viewers notice before and after brushing and whether they connect it to repeated care. Do not interpret brushing time as clinical cleaning difficulty.
6. Log actual observations, failures, costs, repair frequency and photographs as new dated evidence. Decide what to keep with Miu; keep this page marked Planned until a physical test exists.

### Concept / 概念

以退役透明牙套组成微缩双联山水：左侧是染色牙套与真实漱口水构成的真山水；右侧是染色牙套与牙膏白色耙纹构成的枯山水。地貌与牙齿都在缓慢变化，而看似完成的景观仍需要反复维护。这是 [Orthodontic Strata / 口中地层](/CostOfSmile/experiments/orthodontic-strata/) 的延伸，并结合 [Toothbrush Reveal](/CostOfSmile/experiments/toothbrush-reveal/) 的刷拭揭示动作，保留两条旧实验的记录。

### Arrangement / 布置

左侧牙套叠成带岩石色泽的山峰，漱口水拟在独立浅槽中形成河湖；液体与山体是否接触需另试。右侧以牙膏耙出环绕山石的白色纹路；“枯”是景观语言，并不代表牙膏已干燥或稳定。两侧共用按真实治疗阶段排列的原始牙套包装袋背墙，组成半透明医疗档案；缺期留空，隐藏个人资料。前方先设置与液体、档案原件分开的可替换实验片刷拭区，之后再决定是否开放主山体互动。

### Viewer interaction / 观众互动

观众拿提供的牙刷逐渐刷去山体颜色，显露透明牙套及牙齿形状，让“山”与“身体用品”同时可见。先在可替换试片上测试经评估适合此用途的安全颜料，收集残渣并安排牙刷更换与清洁；“可水洗”“无毒”本身不足以证明适合本装置。展示牙刷不可入口，初试不让互动区接触真实漱口水。

### Interpretation / 阐释

牙齿移动转译为地质变化，漱口水与牙膏进入两种对照的山水语言。包装编号呈现治疗的秩序，刷拭则让秩序背后的日常维护劳动显现。**刷褪颜料是清洁反复劳动及困难的艺术隐喻，不等同真实清洁牙齿；刷除时间、阻力不能量化洁牙难度、治疗付出或医学效果。** 观众是否读到这些关系，仍需记录与验证。

### Proposed materials / 拟用材料

退役牙套或干净复制件、匿名化原包装袋、待测试的可移除颜料／涂层、独立容器中的漱口水、用于短期试验的真实牙膏、牙刷、可替换试片托架、残渣盘、可逆支撑或夹具、稳固展台及包装墙支架。模拟水面、稳定仿牙膏材料作为对照选项；若采用必须标注，不假称为真实护理用品。目前不是最终材料清单。

### Feasibility and limitations / 可行性与限制

- 染料可能迁移并污染漱口水和牙膏，改变色泽、质地；需分离对照测试，不能假定颜色只留在山体。
- 真实牙套材质、胶黏兼容性、涂层可逆性未知；刷拭可能造成划痕、雾化和永久染色，先用牺牲试片或复制件与可逆支撑。
- 反复磨损后需要重新上色、干燥、换片或修复；记录可用次数与复位时间。
- 真实漱口水存在泄漏、蒸发、气味、部分配方含酒精、卫生及场地风险。查具体产品资料与场地条件，测试容器兼容、接漏及与电器／观众隔离；效果图开放液池不代表获准展出，必要时比较模拟水面。
- 真实牙膏可能硬化、开裂、发黏、积尘和丢失耙纹，不宜未经测试就敞开长期展示；比较短期可换盘和明确标注的稳定替代品。
- 互动先采用可替换实验片与经评估的安全颜料，安排残渣收集、接触管理和清洁；公共参与的安全、可达性和维护尚未证实。
- 旧牙套展示前需确认与材质兼容的清洁卫生流程；无法确认时用干净复制件。包装姓名、病例号、诊所信息、条码、二维码应遮蔽，同时检查背面、透光与照片中的可读性。
- 检验刷拭力下展台稳定、容器滑移、背墙倾倒、边缘与触及高度，以及日常清洁、补色、换片和维修通道。

以上均作为待实验项目，不声称已经解决。

### Next prototype steps / 下一轮小样

1. 清点少量退役阶段与原袋，记录授权、匿名化、原状及材料未知处，留一件未处理对照。
2. 制作可换片涂层矩阵，记录干燥、刷型、次数、脱色、划伤、残渣和复位时间，保留失败照片。
3. 分开测试颜料与漱口水／牙膏接触；观察颜色、质地、液位及耙纹 24 小时变化，再依据结果决定是否延长到拟展期。
4. 做一组小型左右构图与匿名包装墙片段，测试可逆安装、受力、接漏、触及和清洁，对照并标明真实与模拟材料。
5. 材料和管理方案可接受后才安排小规模监督观看测试，记录刷前刷后的理解，不把刷拭时长等同洁牙困难。
6. 以新日期补入真实观察、成本、故障、修复频率及照片，再与 Miu 讨论保留什么；有实物试验前维持 Planned。
