The dollar sign remains useful as an early study because it immediately connects money and smile, but its meaning may be too fixed.

Alternative arrangements to test include:

- a chronological line;
- a staged grid;
- suspended accumulation;
- a partial sequence that contrasts aligners with ordered packaging.

The central test is whether the form can make **body as project** visible without depending on a large explanatory wall.
