The source material is an electronic calendar recording aligner-change dates.

The aim is not to display calendar screenshots. The artistic opportunity lies in the structure underneath them: dates, stage numbers, repetition, anticipation and the repeated instruction to move the body to its next state.

Possible forms include a long treatment timeline, a stage counter, generative movement through planned dates, or recurring notification-like visual events.

This experiment belongs mainly to **personal / bodily cost** and the body-as-project framework.
