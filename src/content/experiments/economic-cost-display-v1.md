The first display direction concentrated on the visible financial cost of orthodontic treatment: payment, packaging, commercial value and the dollar sign as a spatial or graphic form.

## What works

The treatment is unavoidably a purchase, and the aligners exist within a branded consumer-medical system. Packaging and price remain meaningful materials.

## What is missing

The direction reduces “cost” to money. It does not yet communicate wear time, inconvenience, pressure, self-monitoring or the social desire for straighter teeth. The dollar sign should remain one experiment rather than become the total form of the project.

## Next iteration

Test how the economic layer can sit beside temporal and bodily data without dominating them. Develop visual systems based on stage numbers, hours of wear, repeated actions and appointment rhythms.
